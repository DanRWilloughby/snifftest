# Common questions

## How long does a full run take?

About four minutes on a laptop for a repository of two hundred files. Most of
that is reading the files; the rules themselves are fast.

## Does it send anything anywhere?

Only when you ask for the judgment rules, and only after you have said yes
once. The countable rules run on your machine and make no request at all.

## Can I turn a rule off?

Yes. Copy the rule into your own file, keep its id, and change what it says, or
drop it from your list entirely. The file in your project wins.

## What do I do about a flag I disagree with?

Read the sentence it points at, decide, and move on. The tool has an opinion
about English and you are allowed a different one. If the same rule annoys you
three times in a week, change it rather than ignoring it.

## How do I check the whole repository at once?

1. Run it over the directory rather than a file.
2. Read the first ten lines of the output before you change anything.
3. Fix the ones you agree with.
4. Run it again and see what is left.

## Where do the numbers in the report come from?

From a run over your own writing, seeded with faults the tool knows how to
make. The report says how many, which rule each one belongs to, and what the
whole thing cost.
