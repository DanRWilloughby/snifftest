# Opening the shop

The first person in opens the back door and turns on the lights in the store
room before anything else. The heating takes about twenty minutes to reach the
front of the shop, so it goes on before the till is counted rather than after.
Everything else in this page assumes those two things are already done.

## Before the doors open

| Task | Who | By |
|---|---|---|
| Count the float | Whoever opens | 8:15 |
| Check the fridge log | Whoever opens | 8:20 |
| Bring in the delivery | Two people | 8:40 |

The fridge log matters more than it looks. A reading outside the range has to
be written down at the time, with the number, because a gap in the log is
treated as a failure by the inspector whatever the fridge was actually doing.

## The float

1. Take the bag from the safe and check the seal is unbroken.
2. Count the coin into the tray without emptying the bag onto the counter.
3. Write the total in the book and sign beside it.
4. Put the bag back in the safe before the doors open.

If the total is wrong by less than a pound, write the real number and carry on.
If it is wrong by more, stop, leave the tray as it is, and call the manager.
The point of the rule is that nobody has to decide in the moment how wrong is
too wrong.

> The shop was robbed once, in 1994, and the thing that made the insurance
> straightforward was a float book that had been signed every morning for six
> years.

## Deliveries

- Check the pallet against the note before the driver leaves.
- Anything short goes on the note in pen, and the driver signs it.
- Frozen goods go in first, and the rest can wait on the floor.
- Cardboard goes out flat, not folded, because the recycler charges by volume.

See the [handbook] for what to do when a delivery arrives during opening hours,
and the [inspection guide] for the fridge readings.
